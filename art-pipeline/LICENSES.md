## Important distinctions

1. **The tool license, the model license and the output rights are three different things.**
2. A generated image is not automatically copyrightable in every jurisdiction; it has no author in the human sense. Protect value with curation, licensing terms, bundles and delivery — not by claiming "registered image" status.
3. An NFT is a token on a blockchain. It does not grant copyright by itself. Write the license terms in the product description / metadata.
4. Avoid prompts that reproduce living artists' distinctive styles, real characters, logos or trademarked works.
