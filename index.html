Frequently asked questions

Which networks are supported?
Bitcoin, Ethereum (ERC-20) and USDT on TRC-20. More networks can be added on request.
How do I confirm my payment?
After sending, copy your transaction hash and send it to us through the contact link in the footer. We verify it on-chain.
Do you refund crypto payments?
Payments are final once confirmed on the network. Please double-check the address and amount before sending.
Can I download the image I see on the site?
No. The site shows a reduced, watermarked preview only. After payment is verified, we email the clean full-resolution file to the address you provide.
